Language practice, not a dating app.

Many language apps are known for awkward messages. Speakle is built around the opposite: you stay in control of who can reach you.

  • Block in one tap

    A blocked person can never be matched with you again, in either direction.

  • Report to moderators

    Pick a reason and add details. Reports go to a review queue.

  • Verified email for quick match

    You confirm your email before you can be paired with strangers.

  • A short first call

    Five minutes, then a clear yes or no from both sides. No awkward exits.
